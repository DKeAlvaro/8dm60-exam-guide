CASE · Q1

A slide-level melanoma model is offered for 14,000 mixed skin specimens. Which cases are in scope, what is missing from its output, and what blocks routine use?

CASE · Q2

Which of worklist triage, in-viewer overlay and benign fast-track fit an adjunct intended use? How should slide scores become patient decisions?

CASE · Q3

For 10,000 slides and 5% prevalence, calculate TP/FP/FN, PPV, NPV and flags/day at each threshold. Which point fits assistant vs rule-out?

CASE · Q4

A CE-IVD PD-L1 model changes antibody, stainer, scanner and specimen type. Design site validation around 1% and 50% cut-offs and define ground truth.

CASE · Q5

Case mix changes and a new HER2-low therapy makes 0 vs 1+ clinically important. What drift checks and new validation are required?

CASE · Q6

If “IHC not needed” cases never receive IHC, why is zero disagreement not 100% NPV? Design an ethical evaluation of the prediction paradox.

CASE · Q7

Radiologist–AI agreement rises to 98.6%. Does that prove benefit or calibrated trust? Redesign policy and interface around automation bias.

CASE · Q8

Using the licence, integration, IHC and reading-time figures, does pathology AI pay for itself? Who pays, who benefits, and what non-financial case remains?
