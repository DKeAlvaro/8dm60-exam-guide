# 5 · Clinical workflow integration

## Subtitles

1. Scope, output, and intended use
2. Triage, concurrent assistant, QC, and autonomy
3. Operating points and workload
4. Site-specific and prospective validation
5. Governance, regulation, and accountability
6. Drift, monitoring, and revalidation
7. Human factors and automation bias
8. Economics and who captures value

## Milestones

- [ ] Place a model in the real workflow and intended use.
- [ ] Turn sensitivity/specificity into errors, PPV, NPV, and workload.
- [ ] Match validation evidence to autonomy and deployment site.
- [ ] Specify monitoring, revalidation, accountability, and exit.
- [ ] Calculate a business case and name who owns each risk and benefit.
